# UNSEEN — The World Within 🌌

## Our Idea

We chose **human emotions** as the invisible phenomenon.

Every day, we can see how a person behaves, but we cannot actually see what is happening inside their mind.

A person may smile while feeling sad.
Someone may become angry because of something we cannot see.
Someone may look calm while their mind is full of anxiety.

So we wanted to make these invisible emotions **visible through a fantasy world**.

Instead of simply explaining emotions using text, we created an interactive website where the user can **enter different emotional worlds and experience their stories**.

---

## 🌌 What happens when you open UNSEEN?

The experience starts with:

> **"You can see a person's face.  
> But you cannot see the world they carry inside."**

The user then enters the **Inner World**.

Six emotional worlds appear:

🌸 Happiness  
🌊 Sadness  
🔥 Anger  
🌲 Fear  
⏳ Anxiety  
❤️ Love

The user can click any emotion and enter its world.

---

## ✨ Our Six Emotional Worlds

### 🌸 Happiness — The Garden

The world starts as a quiet, empty garden.

A small golden seed appears.

The seed slowly grows and flowers begin to appear around it.

The idea behind this story is that happiness does not always come from something huge. Sometimes it starts from one small moment that we choose to notice.

**Message:**  
Happiness is something we notice, protect and allow to grow.

---

### 🌊 Sadness — The Ocean

The user enters a dark ocean.

A memory appears like a reflection in the water.

A wave rises and eventually becomes calm.

The story represents how sadness and memories can come in waves. Instead of always trying to fight them, sometimes we need to allow ourselves to feel them.

**Message:**  
Some feelings don't need to be erased. They need to be understood.

---

### 🔥 Anger — The Forge

A small spark becomes a fire.

The fire grows bigger and begins to take control.

The story then changes as the fire is controlled and transformed into strength.

This represents how anger can either destroy something or become energy that helps us change something.

**Message:**  
Anger is energy. What we do with that energy matters.

---

### 🌲 Fear — The Forest

The user enters a dark forest.

A shadow begins following them.

The more they run, the larger the shadow becomes.

Finally, they stop and turn around.

The shadow becomes smaller.

**Message:**  
Sometimes fear becomes bigger when we keep running from it.

---

### ⏳ Anxiety — The Clock

The user enters a room containing clocks.

One clock becomes many.

Different clocks represent different thoughts about the past and future.

The clocks become faster and faster.

Then everything becomes quiet.

Only one clock remains.

**Message:**  
We cannot control every future. We can only return to the moment we are in.

---

### ❤️ Love — The Constellation

Two stars exist far apart in a dark universe.

One star sends a small light.

The other responds.

Slowly, a bridge of light forms between them.

The two worlds are still separate, but now there is a connection between them.

**Message:**  
Love is about building a bridge between two different worlds.

---

## 🎨 Why We Made It This Way

We didn't want to create another website where users simply read:

**Happiness = feeling good**

Instead, we wanted the user to experience:

**Emotion → Fantasy World → Story → Meaning**

The animations represent what the emotion might feel like inside a person's mind.

---

## 🎙️ Story + Voice

Each emotional world contains a short story.

The narration describes exactly what is happening in the animation.

For example:

**Visual:** A seed appears.

**Voice-over:**

> "Then one morning, a tiny golden seed arrived. It was not a miracle. It was one small moment of joy."

The story then continues as the visual world changes.

The narration is synchronized with the story chapters so that the user can **watch and listen at the same time**.

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project in VS Code.
3. Open `index.html`.
4. Run it using **Live Server**.
5. Click **Enter the Inner World**.
6. Choose an emotion.
